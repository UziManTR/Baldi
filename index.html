THE TEACHER
Can you gather all 7 notebooks and escape alive?

HOW TO PLAY

Controls:

  • WASD: Move
  • Mouse: Look Around
  • SHIFT: Run (Consumes Stamina)
  • E: Collect Notebook / Open Exit
  • ESC: Unlock Mouse Cursor

Objective: Collect all 7 Notebooks scattered across the school building and escape through any EXIT door.

Warning: THE TEACHER gets faster and more aggressive with every notebook collected. Running makes noise that alerts him. Manage your stamina wisely!
